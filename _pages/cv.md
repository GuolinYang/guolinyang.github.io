## Profile

PhD researcher in Robotics and AI / Control Systems and Robotics at the University of Manchester, specialising in **world models, model-based reinforcement learning, predictive planning, and safe control for autonomous systems**. My work focuses on compact representations of dynamic, interactive environments and action-conditioned learned dynamics for multi-step prediction and online planning through MPPI and related sampling-based control methods. **Expected PhD completion: December 2026.**

I work primarily with **Python, C++, PyTorch, CARLA, MATLAB/Simulink and closed-loop simulation**, with additional automotive engineering experience at Lotus Technology and NIO.

## Professional & research experience

### PhD Researcher — University of Manchester, UK | 2023–Present

- Developed world-model-based planning methods for autonomous systems, using compact latent dynamics for online multi-step decision-making through MPPI-based predictive control.
- Developed an interaction-aware MBRL framework combining latent representations, recurrent action-conditioned dynamics, pedestrian-interaction modelling, collision-risk prediction, reward/value estimation, and sampling-based planning.
- Built CNN-based BEV and occupancy world-model variants using TD-MPC-style latent predictive-control architectures.
- Developed safety-aware predictive-control methods combining sampling-based MPC, Control Barrier Functions, and risk-sensitive optimisation; reduced collisions by up to **65.7%** relative to a nominal controller in evaluated studies.
- Designed a multi-obstacle discrete-time CBF safety filter, reducing collision rate from **7.8% to 3.8%** across 500 evaluated scenarios with **9.30 ms** reported runtime.
- Implemented and benchmarked autonomous-navigation agents in Python / PyTorch using custom environments and CARLA.
- Supervised Master's dissertation projects in autonomous systems and control.

### Graduate Teaching Assistant — University of Manchester, UK | 2023–Present

Supported teaching and assessment in Robotic Systems, Applied Mechanics & Industrial Robotics, Digital Control, and Model Predictive Control.

### ADAS Engineer — Lotus Technology, Hangzhou, China | 2022

- Supported development of an ADAS speed-limit information feature integrating HD-map and camera-derived inputs.
- Developed C++ software functions for feature logic and vehicle-signal interfaces.
- Used Vector CANoe for CAN-signal monitoring, trace analysis, diagnostics, functional debugging and system integration.
- Conducted DFMEA for the ADAS domain controller, including failure-mode analysis and mitigation actions for assisted steering, automated-driving and HMI functions.

### BMS Software Intern — NIO, Shanghai, China | 2021

- Developed a three-point high-voltage insulation-detection algorithm in MATLAB/Simulink using model-based development / code-generation workflows.
- Worked with DBC files and CAN signals for BMS monitoring, validation and debugging.
- Analysed chamber, bench and vehicle-level data in CANoe, including high-temperature investigation of PTC insulation faults.

### High-Voltage Lead — Hunan University Formula Student Electric Team | 2017–2019

- Led system-level concept design and integration of the EV high-voltage architecture.
- Used MATLAB and AVL CRUISE for powertrain modelling and performance evaluation.
- Applied DFMEA in high-voltage-system development.
- Led a lightweight HV battery structural redesign that achieved a **21% mass reduction** while maintaining performance and safety requirements.
